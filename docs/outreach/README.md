# Outreach

**[Open the tab →](https://lionscraft-io.github.io/risk-cockpit/#outreach)**

Pick a partner and a format; the copy is assembled from the narrative and
tailored to that partner's type. Edit in place, then copy out.

---

## Eight formats

| Format | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Cold intro email** | First contact, no warm route |
| **Warm intro request** | Asking a mutual contact to connect you — includes a forwardable paragraph |
| **Post-meeting follow-up** | Within 24 hours; carries the MoU |
| **MoU outline** | Ten-clause non-binding cooperation agreement |
| **Meeting agenda & questions** | What to actually ask them, timed |
| **One-pager copy** | Tailored to the partner type |
| **Concept note** | For donors and DFIs |
| **Deck spine** | The approved fourteen-slide order |

## Tailoring is by partner type, not name

Each of the nine types carries its own **hook** (why them specifically), **ask**
(what we want), **give** (what is in it for them), and five **discovery
questions**. Choosing a partner pulls in that type's set.

An operator gets *"you already carry the cost of emergency restoration and you
already hold the telemetry that proves it"* and is asked what the last major
event actually cost them. A reinsurer gets *"you already price catastrophe risk
of exactly this shape — what we are adding is what the capital does after the
trigger"* and is asked which trigger types they would genuinely quote on.

Both are true; neither is interchangeable.

## The agenda format carries the guardrails

The meeting agenda ends with them printed on the page, because that is when
they get broken:

- Do not lead with blockchain, tokenisation or AI. Lead with the financial function
- Do not caricature insurance. It works — we are extending what it can express
- Do not claim AI makes contractual decisions
- Do not let them leave thinking we are a Sri Lanka disaster-finance startup

## The MoU is a starting point, not advice

Ten clauses, non-binding except confidentiality, IP and legal effect. It is
deliberately short and deliberately does not commit either side to capital or
capacity.

**Have counsel in the governing-law jurisdiction review it before you send it.**
It is a structure to negotiate from, not a document to sign.

## Everything is editable

The generated text is a starting draft in an editable box. Change it, then copy
it. Nothing is sent from here — this tab produces words, it does not contact
anyone.
