# Programmable Risk Transfer
## Master Narrative, Product and Storytelling Brief

> **Purpose of this document**
>
> This is not a pitch deck. It is the source-of-truth narrative and conceptual brief behind the pitch deck.
>
> Anyone working on the story should understand this document before trying to simplify it into slides, copy, diagrams, product language, grant applications, partner material or website content.
>
> The most important distinction to preserve is:
>
> **We are not fundamentally building a disaster-finance product.**
>
> **We are building a new architecture for risk transfer.**
>
> Disaster and resilience finance is the first application.
>
> Response-Linked ART is the first product expression of that application.
>
> Telecom infrastructure in Sri Lanka is the first bounded implementation.
